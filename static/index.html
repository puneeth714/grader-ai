Stage 02 · The Exam Hall

Students write on real paper with pen — zero screens

Board examinations will always be written on physical paper. Students must build handwriting speed, draw geometry diagrams, and show multi-step math working by hand. We never ask students to type on screens or take online quizzes. The classroom ritual remains 100% sacred.

  • Preserves handwriting stamina required for board examinations
  • Supports geometry drawings, chemical equations, and cursive handwriting
  • Zero student login friction — no devices or tablets required in class
Stage 03 · Fast Correction & ERP Sync

AI pre-checks the handwriting. Teacher approves in 1 minute.

The school clerk drops the bundle of 40 papers into the existing office scanner. In 3 minutes, the entire batch is scanned. Our AI reads each student's handwriting, awards step-by-step marks, and flags exact calculation slips. The teacher opens the screen, reviews the suggested marks, adjusts if needed, and clicks 'Approve'.

  • Clerk scans 40 scripts in 3 minutes — teachers do ZERO manual scanning
  • AI detects formula correctness vs calculation mistakes
  • Zero summation errors — totals are calculated in code, never by hand
  • Approved marks update directly into your School ERP (MyCamu, Entab, etc.)

Common Questions

Everything principals & teachers ask us

Who scans the papers? Do we need to buy expensive scanners?

Zero new hardware is required. You can use any standard automatic feeder (ADF) scanner your school already has in the office or computer lab (Canon, HP, Epson). Any school clerk or lab assistant can scan a bundle of 40 papers in 3 to 5 minutes. Teachers do NOT have to spend time scanning.

Does this replace our teachers' authority?

Never. AI does the heavy initial reading and step-by-step marking; the teacher reviews the evidence and confirms the score in 1–2 minutes instead of 15–20 minutes. The teacher remains the final authority on every single mark.

What about messy handwriting or overwritten numbers?

Our vision models are trained specifically on Indian student handwriting styles (including cursive, diagram labels, and mixed English/Telugu/Hindi numbers). In our live classroom pilot, teacher agreement was over 95%. Any ambiguous answer is immediately flagged for the teacher to inspect.

How do parents react to AI in paper correction?

Parents love it because they finally receive clear proof instead of a raw number. In PTMs, the school gives parents a 1-page Diagnostic Report showing exactly where marks were lost and a 5-question personalized worksheet for their child to practice at home.
